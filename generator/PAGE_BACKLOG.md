# Page backlog (from bucksmontfences.com review, Oct 2026)

Bucksmont (76 FENCE partner, Bucks/Montgomery PA) has 83 pages in a clear hierarchy:
services, materials, fence types, areas, a guides hub, a 20-post blog, projects, team,
how-it-works, reviews, financing. Its strongest pages are long (~900-1,000 words)
local guides: per-township permit pages, pool fence rules "what your inspector will
check", storm damage repair-or-replace, and weather-specific advice. Each links to the
official municipal source and to related service and material pages.

## Rules for building these

- Every code, permit or pool-barrier fact links to the official county/city source,
  checked at build time. No guessing.
- No two sites get the same guide text. Shared topics are rewritten per site, or
  live on one site only.
- Keep the 76 FENCE Tampa / licensing wording rules (licensed in Hillsborough,
  Pasco and Hernando counties; fully insured).
- AI imagery is scenery only, never presented as our installs; projects use real photos.
- New pages go through review before publishing.
